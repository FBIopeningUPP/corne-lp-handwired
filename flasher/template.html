Corne LP Flasher

Write the ZMK firmware straight onto a SuperMini nRF52840 in bootloader mode, then press every key to check it. Works in Chrome or Edge on a computer.


← Corne LP home

1 · Choose the firmware

2 · Put the board in bootloader mode

  • Bare board

    Plug it in by USB-C, then short RST to GND twice, quickly, with tweezers.

  • Built into the case

    Double-press the reset button through the pinhole in the base with a paperclip.

  • Already running ZMK

    Hold NUM + SYM for the ADJ layer and press the bootloader key on that half.

A drive called NICENANO appears and the board's LED pulses. That is the drive you pick in step 3.

3 · Flash

Pick the NICENANO drive when asked. Allow Chrome to edit it.
Order for a new build, and what to do if the halves won't talk
  • Flash left onto one board and right onto the other. Mark them.
  • Power both at the same time: they pair with each other on their own, then the left half shows up in your computer's Bluetooth list as Corne LP.
  • Only left-half keys work in the tester? Flash settings reset onto both boards, then flash left and right again and power them up together.
  • No NICENANO drive? Try another cable (some are charge-only) and a quicker double tap.

4 · Test every key

Connect over Bluetooth (or USB on the left half), click this page, and press each key once. Keys light up as they arrive. NUM and SYM light when a key from their layer comes through.
